1.1 LECTURE - The Challenge of Evaluating AI Agents
    
    A. Why Traditional Testing Falls Short
       • The Deterministic Testing Paradigm
         - Same input always produces same output
         - Behavior is explicitly programmed and predictable
         - Success measured through exact string matching
       • How AI Agents Break the Paradigm
         - Non-determinism through temperature and sampling
         - Emergent behavior and autonomous decisions
         - Context dependency on retrieved documents
         - Qualitative assessment requirements
    
    B. The Agent Evaluation Challenge
       • Multi-Dimensional Complexity
         - Multi-step reasoning evaluation
         - Tool calling accuracy assessment
         - Retrieved context relevance
         - Conversation coherence tracking
       • Safety and Real-World Variability
         - Safety and alignment requirements
         - Real-world variability handling
         - Edge case management
    
    C. Evaluation as a Continuous Process
       • The Continuous Evaluation Cycle
         - Development phase evaluation
         - Pre-deployment validation
         - Production monitoring
         - Continuous improvement loops
       • Why Continuous Evaluation Matters
         - Rapid feedback loops during development
         - Comprehensive validation before deployment
         - Ongoing monitoring in production
    
    D. Preparing for Evaluation
       • Why Preparation Matters
         - Purposeful and reproducible evaluation
         - Addressing non-deterministic behavior
       • Designing Your Evaluation Dataset
         - Coverage of expected use cases
         - Edge case identification
         - Ground truth preparation
       • Operational Setup Requirements
         - MLflow experiments and runs configuration
         - Tagging and versioning strategy
         - Result tracking and comparison

       • Defining Prompt Engineering
         - Tactical discipline for optimizing LLM outputs
         - Few-Shot Prompting techniques
         - Persona Adoption strategies
       • Reasoning vs. Non-Reasoning Models
         - Non-Reasoning Models (Llama 3, GPT-4o)
